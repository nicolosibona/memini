Specification – 12 V / 5 A switching power supply

Nordlys Electronics ApS · Engineering · Doc. ENG-SP-031 rev. B
Part no. PS-1205
Date: 2026-11-18

Requirements for the external power supply of the NL-200 lighting controller. All values at 25 °C ambient, full load, unless stated.

#Requirement
1Input voltage: 100–240 VAC
2Output voltage: 12 V DC
3Output current: ≥ 5 A
4Ripple & noise: ≤ 120 mV p-p
5Efficiency: ≥ 88 %
6No-load power: ≤ 0.3 W
7Operating temperature: -20 to +70 °C
8Output capacitor: 1000 µF, rated voltage ≥ 25 V, 105 °C
9Protections: OVP, OCP, SCP, OTP
10Certifications: CE, RoHS, FCC
11Cable length: 1.5 m
12MTBF: ≥ 50,000 h

Any change of component (including equivalent substitutes) requires written approval before production.
